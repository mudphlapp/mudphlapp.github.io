The Old Man Trading Desk

The Old Man Trading Desk is a live YouTube show that follows the SPX options trades of "The Old Man." He farmed and ranched his whole life and has traded options for 30 years, always probability first. Four AI-voiced desk hosts walk through each position: Atlas (anchor), Nova (bull), Hollis (bear) and Vega (numbers and SPX gamma). Every trade is shown on a one-contract basis. No account details are ever shown.
